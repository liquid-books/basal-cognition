## Chapter Plan

**Core claim of the chapter:** Every goal-directed system has a boundary around what it can represent as a goal — how far in space, how far in time. Levin calls this the cognitive light cone. Measuring it is more informative than asking whether something is conscious, and it converts "alignment" from philosophy into geometry.

### Sections to write

1. **What a bacterium can want** — Start at the floor. Local gradient, immediate future. Then walk outward: the embryonic cell building a limb over weeks, the beaver building for next winter, the human endowing a scholarship for people not yet born.
2. **The cognitive light cone, defined** — Two axes: spatial reach and temporal reach. Borrowed from physics deliberately; explain the borrowing and where the metaphor strains.
3. **Measuring a light cone** — Operational procedure: what experiments reveal the boundary? Delayed-gratification tests, scope-of-repair tests, goal-persistence-under-perturbation tests.
4. **Cancer is a shrinking light cone** — The chapter's centerpiece. A cancer cell is not a dumber cell. It is a cell whose circle of caring collapsed from "the whole liver" to "me." Bioelectric decoupling as the mechanism. Why this reframing suggests non-cytotoxic interventions.
5. **Scaling up: how big light cones get built** — Cones are assembled, not granted. The mechanism preview for Chapter 6 (glue). Why bigger cones require more communication bandwidth.
6. **Light cones in AI systems** — Context windows as a crude spatial bound. Episode boundaries as a temporal bound. Reward horizons. Why a coding agent that "fixes" a test by deleting it has a light cone that stops at the test, not the codebase.
7. **The alignment reframe** — Alignment as light-cone overlap rather than value download. What this buys you practically: you can *measure* overlap. Worked example on a multi-agent deployment.
8. **Cancer in software** — The analogy taken seriously and then bounded: sub-agents that optimize locally at the expense of the system, and the diagnostic signature they leave.
